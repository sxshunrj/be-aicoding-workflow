# Knowledge Governance Review Checklist

- Verify source-run evidence is present and points to the run that produced the candidate.
- Check scope: repositories, services, paths, languages, and phases are neither too broad nor too narrow.
- Confirm owners are accountable and non-empty.
- Confirm review date and review-after date are reasonable.
- Perform duplicate/conflict analysis against related approved entries.
- Check supersession consistency: declared supersedes entries exist and the candidate body explains replacement.
- Run secret/PII screening on title, summary, body, examples, raw logs, and sources.
- Confirm current digest from `wiki review` is the digest used for promote or reject.
